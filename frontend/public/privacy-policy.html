CAREER CROX · UPDATED 28 OCTOBER 2026

Privacy and data use

Career Crox is a workplace recruitment CRM and calling companion for authorised company staff. The Android companion requires pairing with an authenticated Career Crox workspace. It is not an anonymous call-history collection service.

Information used for work

The app uses the account identity, device pairing identifier, downloaded candidate details, work notes, tasks and work-call records needed for the paired CRM. With your explicit agreement and Android permissions, it checks call history to verify work-call numbers, direction, start time, result and duration. Reports sent to the CRM are limited to downloaded CRM numbers and numbers explicitly called through the app. Unrelated phone-call records are excluded from saved and shared reports.

Approximate location is optional. When you enable it separately, a recent approximate location can accompany work-call updates. You can turn it off in Settings.

What the app does not collect

This Android source does not request SMS access, address-book contacts, Accessibility access or permission to record calls. It does not collect call audio. It contains no advertising SDK. Android notification permission is used for the app's own visible work notifications, not to read other apps' notifications.

Who can access reports

Work records are sent over HTTPS to the Career Crox workspace you select and pair with. Your account and authorised managers or team members can access them according to that workspace's permissions. The workplace operates and controls its CRM records.

Your controls

You can decline phone permission and still connect to view available work features. Verified calling requires the stated permissions. You can stop call sharing, turn off optional location or unpair the phone in Settings. You can also revoke Android permissions. Unpairing stops further collection and removes the active pairing; it does not erase work records already saved in the company CRM.

Storage and retention

Unsynced work reports and notes can remain in the app's private storage until acknowledged by the CRM or managed through the workplace's data process. Local call-history previews cover recent work records. Server records, audit history and backups follow the company's retention and access rules. Ask your company manager for the applicable schedule and requests for access, correction or removal.

Contact and data requests

Contact your Career Crox workplace manager through your company's usual email, phone or team-chat support channel. If you are using the Google Play version, the publisher's direct contact details are also available under Developer contact on its Google Play listing. Include your workspace and account name when requesting help; do not send passwords, pairing tokens or private signing keys.

App review and permission declarations

Work-call history is part of the corporate CRM calling function. Distribution on Google Play is subject to Google's review, including the business/enterprise CRM Call Log permission declaration where applicable. This page does not represent a Google approval.
